<h2 align="center">Building Reliable Cloud Infrastructure</h2>

<p align="center">
Designing scalable, automated, and resilient cloud platforms with DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering practices.
</p>

<br>

I work on the infrastructure most people never think about — until it breaks.

Currently, I'm a **Cloud Infrastructure & Operations Engineer at Cloud4C (Capgemini)**, supporting **1,400+ enterprise environments** across hybrid cloud and virtualization platforms including VMware vSphere, Hyper-V, XenServer, and KVM.

Over the past few years, my focus has shifted from traditional infrastructure operations toward **DevOps** and **Site Reliability Engineering**, where I enjoy solving problems through automation, Infrastructure as Code, Kubernetes, GitOps, and observability rather than repetitive manual work.

Outside of production support, I build production-inspired cloud-native projects on AWS to deepen my understanding of modern infrastructure. My current learning journey focuses on Kubernetes, CI/CD, GitOps, monitoring, and contributing to the CNCF ecosystem through Meshery.
